# Store Policies & FAQ

> Replace this file with your store's real policies. The whole file is loaded
> into the chatbot's context at boot (and prompt-cached), so the assistant can
> answer policy questions accurately. Keep it factual and well-structured —
> headings help the model cite the right section.

## Shipping

- Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day.
- Standard shipping (3–5 business days) is free on orders over $50.
- Expedited (1–2 business days) is available at checkout for a flat fee.
- We ship to the US and Canada. International shipping is not available yet.

## Returns & Exchanges

- Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery, in original condition.
- Start a return from your account's Order History, or ask here to be connected
  to a human who can help.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of
  us receiving the return.
- Final-sale items (marked on the product page) cannot be returned.

## Sizing

- Most apparel runs true to size; see the size chart on each product page.
- If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

## Payments

- We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
- Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address.

## Order Changes & Cancellations

- Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placement — ask here and
  we'll connect you to a human right away, since the order may already be in
  fulfillment.

## Contact

- For anything the chatbot can't resolve, it can escalate you to our support
  team, who reply within 1 business day.
